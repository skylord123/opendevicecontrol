opendevicecontrol-sensors: OpenDeviceControl's hardware sensor service (Windows)

  --install     install or update the service (administrator): copies it to
                %ProgramFiles%\OpenDeviceControl\Sensors, installs PawnIO if needed
                (PawnIO_setup.exe next to this exe), and starts it
  --uninstall   stop and remove the service (administrator); PawnIO stays
  --status      what the running service reads
  --console     serve from this console instead (administrator; testing)

The service runs as LocalSystem and reads the CPU's sensors through the
PawnIO driver, which only administrators may open. OpenDeviceControl reads the results
over the local, read-only pipe \\.\pipe\opendevicecontrol-sensors.
Log: %ProgramData%\OpenDeviceControl\sensors.log
